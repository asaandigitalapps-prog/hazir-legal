Hazir · AsaanDigital Applications

Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026 · Last updated 6 November 2026

1. Who we are

Hazir is a service by AsaanDigital Applications, Pakistan ("we", "us"). Hazir is an AI assistant that businesses (for example car workshops, salons and shops) connect to their WhatsApp Business number so it can answer their customers, share prices and pass conversations to staff.

When you message a business that uses Hazir, that business decides why and how your messages are used (it is the data controller), and we process your data on its behalf (we are the processor). For business owners and staff who use Hazir accounts, we are the controller of their account data.

Contact: asaandigital.apps@gmail.com

2. You are talking to an AI

Replies may be written by an AI assistant. If you ask, it will tell you it is an AI, and you can ask for a person at any time. The assistant only states prices, availability and policies that the business has provided; final prices and bookings are confirmed by the business.

3. Data we process

WhoData
Customers who message a businessWhatsApp phone number, WhatsApp profile name, the content of your messages (text, photos, voice notes, documents, location you choose to share), message times and delivery status, booking or order details you provide, ratings and comments you give after a service, and your opt-out choice.
Business owners and staffName, business details, phone number, email, login information, settings, and actions taken in Hazir (for example approvals).
EveryoneTechnical and security records such as an audit log of actions, error logs, and IP addresses of requests to our servers.

Voice notes: when you send a voice note, it is converted into text automatically. We keep the text, not the recording. Automatic transcripts can contain mistakes, so the assistant repeats important details back to you before acting on them.

We do not ask for sensitive data such as health, religious or financial account details, and we ask you not to send it. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Why we use it

  • To reply to your messages on behalf of the business, look up its prices and services, and pass your conversation to its staff when needed.
  • To record bookings, orders and requests for the business.
  • To ask you once after a finished service how it went (at most about once a month, never after you opt out), and give your rating and comment to the business so it can improve and contact you if something went wrong. Answering is optional.
  • To respect your choices, such as STOP to opt out.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and spam, fix errors, and keep records required by law.

Legal bases: performing the service you or the business requested, the business's legitimate interest in answering its customers, your consent where required, and legal obligations.

5. Service providers

We use carefully selected providers who process data only to run Hazir, under contracts that protect it:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform) — delivers WhatsApp messages. WhatsApp's own privacy policy also applies to your use of WhatsApp.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — the AI model that drafts replies. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through the API is not used to train its models.
  • OpenAI (speech-to-text API) — converts voice notes into text so the assistant can understand them. Hazir does not store the audio; only the text transcript is kept, like any other message. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train its models by default.
  • Cloud hosting and database providers — store data and run our servers.
  • Google (Google Sheets) — only if the business chooses to mirror its records to its own Google account.

These providers may process data outside your country (for example in the United States or the European Union). We rely on their contractual safeguards for such transfers.

6. How long we keep it

  • Conversation and booking records: as long as the business uses Hazir and up to 12 months after your last message, unless the business must keep them longer by law.
  • Ratings and comments: 12 months.
  • Security and audit records: up to 24 months.
  • When a business stops using Hazir, its data is deleted or returned within 90 days.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), verify that incoming WhatsApp messages genuinely come from Meta, restrict access to authorised staff, keep secrets out of our code and logs, keep an audit log of actions, and keep each business's data separate from other businesses.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law and applicable Pakistani law), you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its use, or receive a copy. Send STOP to the business's WhatsApp number to stop messages; send START to resume. To delete your data, see our Data Deletion Instructions. We reply within 30 days. Requests about a business's records may be passed to that business, as it controls them.

9. Children

Hazir is not intended for people under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version; important changes will be announced on this page.
