Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026 · Last updated 13 November 2026

1. Who we are

Hazir is a service by AsaanDigital Applications, Pakistan ("we", "us"). Hazir is an AI assistant that small businesses (for example salons, beauty parlours, car workshops, repair shops, restaurants, home kitchens, pharmacies, tailors, gyms, home service and delivery businesses) connect to their WhatsApp Business number. It answers their customers, shares prices, takes bookings and orders, asks for payments to the business's own accounts and passes conversations to staff.

When you message a business that uses Hazir, that business decides why and how your messages are used (it is the data controller), and we process your data on its behalf (we are the processor). For business owners and staff who use Hazir, we are the controller of their account data.

Contact: info@asaandigitalsolutions.com

2. You are talking to an AI

Replies may be written by an AI assistant. If you ask, it will tell you it is an AI, and you can ask for a person at any time. The assistant only states prices, availability and policies that the business has provided; final prices and bookings are confirmed by the business. It does not give medical, legal or financial advice.

4. Why we use it

  • To reply to your messages on behalf of the business, look up its prices and services, and pass your conversation to its staff when needed.
  • To make and manage bookings, orders, repairs, classes and memberships, and to send reminders and updates about them.
  • To send you a chat message when your call to the business's WhatsApp number was missed, so you can be helped in the chat.
  • To ask for a payment with the amount and the business's own account details. Hazir never receives or holds your money: you pay the business directly, and the business confirms when it has received the payment.
  • To ask you once after a finished service how it went (at most about once a month, never after you opt out), and give your rating and comment to the business. Answering is optional.
  • For business owners: to set up and run the business's account, send owner alerts, sign the owner in to the dashboard, confirm a change of owner number with a one-time code, and send Hazir invoices, payment reminders and receipts on WhatsApp.
  • To apply the business's offers to your booking, and to recognise you as a returning customer (for example to suggest your usual service), using your earlier bookings with that business only.
  • Only if you said yes: to send you the business's special offers on WhatsApp, at most one a week. Reply STOP OFFERS to stop offers, or STOP to stop all messages.
  • To respect your choices, such as STOP to opt out.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and spam, fix errors, and keep records required by law.

Legal bases: performing the service you or the business requested, the business's legitimate interest in answering its customers, your consent where required (for example for offer messages and for health information you choose to share), and legal obligations.

5. Cookies

The business dashboard and setup pages use only cookies needed for them to work: a sign-in cookie (ends after 12 hours or when you sign out) and a language choice. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and these pages load no third-party scripts.

6. Service providers

We use carefully selected providers who process data only to run Hazir, under contracts that protect it:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform) — delivers WhatsApp messages and media. WhatsApp's own privacy policy also applies to your use of WhatsApp.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — the AI model that drafts replies. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through the API is not used to train its models.
  • OpenAI (speech-to-text API) — converts voice notes into text. Hazir does not store the audio; only the text is kept, like any other message. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through the API is not used to train its models by default.
  • Cloud hosting, database and network providers (for example our server host and Cloudflare) — store data, run our servers and protect connections to them.
  • Payment providers chosen by the business (for example JazzCash or Easypaisa) — only if the business uses their payment links; you then pay through that provider under its own terms.
  • Google (Google Sheets) — only if a business chooses to copy its records to its own Google account.

These providers may process data outside your country (for example in the United States, the European Union or Asia). We rely on their contractual safeguards for such transfers.

7. How long we keep it

  • Conversations, bookings, orders, repairs, classes, memberships and enquiries: up to 12 months after they ended or after your last message, unless the business must keep them longer by law. A customer record with nothing left is then deleted.
  • Ratings and comments: 12 months.
  • Payment records: 24 months, for the business's accounts.
  • Tailoring measurements: 24 months after they were last updated, or earlier if you ask.
  • Security and audit records: up to 24 months.
  • Unfinished business setup forms: deleted 30 days after the setup link expires. One-time codes: deleted within a day.
  • When a business stops using Hazir, its data is returned to it on request and deleted within 90 days. Hazir invoices, payments made to AsaanDigital, our account notes and the owner's feedback to us are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires; they do not include the business's customers' data.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), verify that incoming WhatsApp messages genuinely come from Meta, keep each business's data separate from other businesses at the database level, restrict access to authorised people, use one-time sign-in links and codes stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, keep secrets out of our code and logs, and keep an audit log of actions.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law and applicable Pakistani law), you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its use, or receive a copy. Send STOP to the business's WhatsApp number to stop messages; send START to resume. To delete your data, see our Data Deletion Instructions. We reply within 30 days. Requests about a business's records may be passed to that business, as it controls them. Business owners can correct their business details, services, team and payment accounts themselves in the dashboard settings.

10. Children

Hazir is not intended for people under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
