Data Deletion Instructions

Effective 5 November 2026 · Last updated 11 November 2026

You can ask us to delete the personal data Hazir holds about you at any time.

How to request deletion

  • Customers, by email: write to asaandigital.apps@gmail.com with the subject "Hazir data deletion request". Include the WhatsApp number you used and the name of the business you messaged.
  • Customers, through the business: you can also ask the business you messaged; it will pass the request to us.
  • Business owners: email us from the address you gave us, or message us from your registered owner WhatsApp number, to close your account and delete your business data. You can ask for a copy of your data first. Hazir invoices and payments you made to AsaanDigital are kept for our accounts as the law requires.
  • If you used Facebook Login with Hazir, you can also remove Hazir in Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites, then email us as above to delete the data we hold.

What happens next

  1. We confirm the request comes from the owner of the number or account (for example by a WhatsApp or email confirmation).
  2. Within 30 days we delete, at every business that uses Hazir: your messages and chat history, profile name, bookings, orders, repairs, classes and memberships, ratings and comments, measurements, vehicle details, missed call records, pending payment requests and any alerts about you, and tell the business concerned.
  3. Records of payments you actually made stay with the business for its accounts and tax records, but without your phone number, name, chat or screenshots.
  4. We keep a minimal record that the deletion was done, with a reference code that does not contain your number.
  5. We confirm when deletion is complete and give you the reference code.

For business accounts we delete the business, its services, team, settings and its customers' data within 90 days of the request (usually much sooner), after returning a copy if you asked for one.

Stop messages without deleting

Send STOP to the business's WhatsApp number to stop receiving messages. Send START to resume.

Messages also exist in your own WhatsApp app and on the business's WhatsApp account; you can delete those in WhatsApp. See also our Privacy Policy.
