Data Deletion Instructions

Effective 5 November 2026

You can ask us to delete the personal data Hazir holds about you at any time.

How to request deletion

  • By email: write to asaandigital.apps@gmail.com with the subject "Hazir data deletion request". Include the WhatsApp number you used and the name of the business you messaged.
  • Business owners: email us from the address on your Hazir account, or ask through the Hazir owner app, to delete your account and your business data.
  • If you used Facebook Login with Hazir, you can also remove Hazir in Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites, then email us as above to delete the data we hold.

What happens next

  1. We confirm the request comes from the owner of the number or account (for example by a WhatsApp or email confirmation).
  2. Within 30 days we delete your messages, profile name, booking details and other personal data from our systems, and tell the business concerned.
  3. We keep only what the law requires (for example invoices a business must keep for tax), and a minimal record that the deletion was done.
  4. We confirm by email when deletion is complete.

Stop messages without deleting

Send STOP to the business's WhatsApp number to stop receiving messages. Send START to resume.

Messages also exist in your own WhatsApp app and on the business's WhatsApp account; you can delete those in WhatsApp. See also our Privacy Policy.
